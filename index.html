The autopilot

The bar at the top of the flight screen points the nose for you, so you can concentrate on the throttle.

OffYou steer by hand HoldLocks the heading you have right now ProgradeNose along your direction of travel. Burn to go faster and raise your orbit RetrogradeNose against your travel. Burn to slow down, drop out of orbit, or land AwayNose straight up, away from the world below
